# Onboarding questionnaire

The first time someone opens their copy of the tracker, the **Onboarding**
tab walks them through one questionnaire. Everything is optional except a
name and at least one target job title, everything can be edited later, and
nothing leaves the person's own tracker database.

When they finish, their answers become their profile: the fit score is
weighted around their target roles and locations, the daily lead sweep knows
what to look for, and application forms can be filled from standing answers
they chose to store — never from guesses.

## 1. Resume first

- Upload a resume file (PDF, DOCX, TXT, or MD). Text-based files are read
  right in the browser; for a PDF/DOCX the file name is saved and the person
  pastes their resume text (or a summary) so the tracker can use it.
- LinkedIn profile URL, portfolio/website URL.
- A short headline: the one line they'd put under their name.

## 2. Connect email

- Which provider: Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, other, or not yet.
- Connected, not yet, or do it later.
- What the assistant may look for (each one separately on/off): job-alert
  emails, interview invitations, decisions/rejections, recruiter messages.
- How often to scan, and whether they've set up a label/filter for job mail.

Connecting the account itself happens in their assistant's connector
settings — this questionnaire records the choice and the permissions, so
there's a written record of exactly what was agreed.

## 3. Job-alert emails: LinkedIn and Indeed

For each of LinkedIn and Indeed:

- Are job-alert emails already switched on? (Yes / Not yet / Help me)
- Which job titles the alerts watch, which locations, and how often the
  email digest arrives (daily is best for a daily sweep).

Setup path the questionnaire shows: LinkedIn -> Jobs -> Job alerts ->
create alerts for each target title + location, email frequency Daily.
Indeed -> Job Alerts -> add the same titles + locations, daily emails.
It also asks about company career-page alerts and any field-specific
boards, so no alert source is silently missing.

## 4. About you

Name, city/state, timezone, phone (optional), and — only if they want
application forms pre-filled — the address to use on forms, stored as an
explicit opt-in.

## 5. What you're targeting

- Primary lane (their words, e.g. "your target field") and the exact job
  titles that count as a match.
- A second, "secondary" lane for income-while-you-search roles, if any.
- Seniority target, industries/specialties, dream companies, companies
  to avoid, and hard deal-breakers.

## 6. Where you'll work

Home base, locations they'd accept, states on/off, remote/hybrid/on-site,
willing to relocate, maximum travel %, commute radius, on-call tolerance.

## 7. Money

Target total compensation, minimum base, minimum total they'd accept,
and free-text pay notes (e.g. "no pure-commission roles").

## 8. Experience snapshot

Years of aligned experience, top skills, education, certifications,
languages. Pre-fill it from the resume in step 1 and fix what's wrong —
this is what fit scoring and form answers draw on.

## 9. Standing application answers

The questions almost every application asks: work authorization,
sponsorship, driver's license, shift availability, earliest start,
restrictive covenants (yes/no/not sure). Self-ID/EEO answers are
**optional and stored only on an explicit opt-in**, with the warning
that they live in the person's own database and can be deleted any time.

## 10. Warm network

Who already knows them at target companies, whether they're happy to ask
for referrals, and a weekly outreach target. Contacts themselves live in
the Key Contacts tab; this step captures the strategy.

## 11. Automation and safety preferences

- Approval mode: drafts only, approve each submission, or auto-submit
  roles that pass their fit gates with every required field known.
- Outreach rule (fixed, shown for acknowledgement): drafts are never sent
  without their exact-text approval; they copy and send themselves.
- Lead sweep frequency and weekly check-in day.

## 12. Privacy

Where their data lives (their own database), that it is never shared or
published by the template, export/backup preference, and a final review
screen listing anything still missing before they hit "Finish" — which
marks onboarding complete and turns their answers into tracker settings.
